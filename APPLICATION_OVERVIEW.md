# 🎵 Audio Splitter App - Complete Application Overview

## 🎯 What This Application Does

The Audio Splitter App is a **professional-grade, cross-platform digital audio distribution system** that transforms any device into a powerful audio broadcasting hub, capable of streaming high-quality audio to multiple devices simultaneously with **studio-level synchronization**.

## 🚀 Core Functionality

### 📡 **Digital Audio Broadcasting**
- **Host Mode**: Transform your device into an audio server that captures and broadcasts audio
- **Client Mode**: Connect to host devices to receive synchronized audio streams
- **Multi-Source Audio**: Stream from microphone, system audio, media files, or online streams
- **Real-Time Processing**: Live audio capture, processing, and distribution

### 🔗 **Universal Connectivity**
- **WiFi Network Streaming**: Connect devices on the same network automatically
- **Bluetooth Audio**: Pair and stream to Bluetooth headphones, speakers, and devices
- **Cross-Platform**: Works seamlessly between Android, iOS, and Windows devices
- **Auto-Discovery**: Automatically find and connect to available hosts

### ⚡ **Professional Synchronization**
- **Sub-50ms Latency**: Professional-grade audio synchronization across all devices
- **Automatic Drift Correction**: Maintains perfect sync even during long sessions
- **Adaptive Buffering**: Intelligent buffering based on network conditions
- **Quality Optimization**: Automatically adjusts quality for optimal performance

## 💡 **How It Works**

### **As a Host Device:**
1. **Select Audio Source**: Choose microphone, system audio, or media files
2. **Configure Settings**: Set audio quality, network port, and preferences
3. **Start Broadcasting**: Begin hosting and wait for device connections
4. **Manage Connections**: Monitor and control connected devices
5. **Stream Audio**: Real-time audio distribution with perfect synchronization

### **As a Client Device:**
1. **Discover Hosts**: Automatically find available audio hosts on the network
2. **Connect**: Join a host's audio stream with one tap
3. **Choose Output**: Select device speakers or connected Bluetooth audio
4. **Enjoy Synchronized Audio**: Receive perfectly synchronized audio
5. **Control Experience**: Adjust volume and audio settings individually
